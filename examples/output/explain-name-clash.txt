name-clash

level: error
kind: dictionary

what it looks at: one name or alias used by two different metrics (compared without case and without extra spaces): the classic way two reports show two numbers under one name; a deprecated metric that names the other in replaced_by is left out

why it exists: Requiring a name or an alias to belong to one metric is a best practice of this tool; no standard sets it; a reader who sees the name in two reports cannot tell which definition stands behind the number

what to change: Give each metric a name of its own that says what is different (for example "Revenue (booked)" and "Revenue (recognised)"), and remove the alias from the metric that does not own it.

what it cannot see: It compares the written names and aliases. It cannot tell two names for one measure ("Sales" and "Revenue") from two measures, and it cannot see a name that is only used in a dashboard.

This check is a best practice of this tool; no standard sets it. The tool is a read of the wording and the structure of a metric dictionary, and of a list of reports, from plain files; it does not run a formula, does not look at any data and does not know whether a definition is right or a number is correct, and it is neither a data quality tool nor an audit.
